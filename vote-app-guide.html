The setup · 5 steps

From install to counting

1

Download the app from the voting page

On blinksunited.com/voting (signed in, with a scrobbler linked), open the VMA Vote Counter block and tap Download Android app (.apk). When it finishes, open the file.

2

Install it (it's a sideloaded app)

Android asks permission the first time: allow Install unknown apps for your browser. You'll also see a Google Play Protect notice — that shows for any app not from the Play Store. Tap Install anyway → Got it. It's safe; it just hasn't been seen by Google before. Chrome's “this file could harm your device” line is the same routine warning — tap Download anyway.

3

Link your Blinks United account

Open the app and tap 🔗 Link. It opens your normal browser — sign in with the same account you use on the site (X / Google / Discord / email all work here). The browser hands you back to the app, and the header flips to ● Linked.

4

Vote inside the app

Tap 🗳 VOTE — vote.mtv.com opens right inside the app. Vote for BLACKPINK (Best K-Pop) and LISA (Best Pop / Best K-Pop) as usual. Each successful vote is counted and sent to your board automatically; the header count goes up, and any unsent votes show ⏳ syncing until they land.

5

Track the accounts you've used

Tap 👤 Accounts used today to see each account you've voted with and how many of the two fan categories it's covered — 2/2 (done) or 1/2 (one to go). Resets daily. Tap 📊 BOARD to open your live /voting standing in the browser.

Under the hood

What the app does

🗳️
Counts as you vote

Reads each successful vote you cast on vote.mtv.com inside the app and logs the BLACKPINK/LISA ones to your board.

🔗
One-tap linking

Sign in once in your real browser; the token comes straight back to the app. No copy-paste.

👤
Accounts & categories

Every account you use today with a 2/2 or 1/2 badge for the two fan-voted categories. Local to your phone.

⏳
Never loses a vote

Counted votes retry until they reach the board; a "syncing" marker shows anything still in flight.

📊
Board at a tap

Opens your live /voting standing in your browser, already signed in — no second login.

🛡️
Count-only, by design

It only observes votes you cast yourself — it never votes for you, so it stays within VMA rules.

🍎

Android only

iPhone can't sideload apps, so there's no iOS version — on an iPhone, vote on vote.mtv.com in Safari and log your total on /voting, or use a computer with the browser extension. The app installs on Android 7.0+ and updates in place when a new version is posted.
