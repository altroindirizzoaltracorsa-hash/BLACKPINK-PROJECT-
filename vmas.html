BLACKPINK & the VMAs · through the years

BLACKPINK are the most-nominated female K-pop act in VMA history — 16 nods and counting — and every year they keep making it.

2020
🏆 Song of Summer · BLACKPINK — “How You Like That”
Their first-ever VMA win — and the first Asian girl group to take the category.
2022
🏆 Best Metaverse Performance · BLACKPINK — “The Virtual” (PUBGM)
Winners of the inaugural award in the category.
🏆 Best K-Pop · LISA — “LALISA”
The first solo K-pop artist to win a Moon Person.
2023
🏆 Group of the Year · BLACKPINK
The first girl group in 24 years to win the award.
🏆 Best Choreography · BLACKPINK — “Pink Venom”
2024
🏆 Best K-Pop · LISA — “Rockstar”
The first artist ever to win Best K-Pop twice.
2025
🏆 Best Group · BLACKPINK
Their second win in the group category, after 2023.
🏆 Song of the Year · ROSÉ — “APT.” with Bruno Mars
The first K-pop artist ever to win Song of the Year.
🏆 Best K-Pop · LISA — “Born Again” with Doja Cat & RAYE
Back-to-back — her second Best K-Pop in a row.
⭐ A VMAs first · JENNIE · JISOO · ROSÉ · LISA
The first K-pop group with all four members nominated for the same award — every one of them up for Best K-Pop.
2026
You are here
Nominated: BLACKPINK “JUMP” · LISA “Dream” · JISOO × ZAYN “Eyes Closed”
🏆 Best Pop · LISA — “Dream”
The only female act in VMAs history to win both Best K-Pop and Best Pop.
⭐ A new record · LISA
Four more nods this year make her the most-nominated K-pop soloist in VMA history — 10 in total.
