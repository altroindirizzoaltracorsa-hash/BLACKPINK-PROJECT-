Some awards make you vote in every category before anything is submitted. When two fandoms are nominated in different categories, each can fill the other's slot at no cost to itself — two votes out of one ballot. This page is where those trades live: who we are working with, what to vote on each side, and how long is left.

How a trade works
  • Nothing is given up. A ballot that will not submit until every category is filled means you were going to put something in that category anyway. Putting the other fandom's nominee there costs you nothing — it is a slot, not a choice between them and us.
  • It only works across categories. If both acts are nominated in the same category there is no trade to make, only a fight. Every collab here names the exact categories so you can see that for yourself.
  • The numbers are not two teams. Everyone in a collab votes the whole ballot, so a vote cannot be traced to a fandom — nothing here knows whether the person who picked BLACKPINK is a blink or not. The two figures are what each act received out of the collab, and they sit apart roughly in the ratio of the slot counts.
  • Every pick is worth one. A pass through the ballot is as many votes as it carried, and no category is worth more than another — the member in one is not competing with the member in the next.
  • We count what people log here, and say so. It is votes recorded through this site or the vote counter extension: self-reported — accurate and effortless, but still what people tell us — so it reads logged, never verified.
  • A trade vote is never a BLACKPINK vote. Picks for a partner's nominee are kept out of your BLACKPINK totals, your rank and your badges. They belong to the collab and nowhere else.
🤝 Propose a collab

Run a voting page for another act? If it is nominated in different categories of an award BLACKPINK or a member is in, there is a trade to make — tell us about it. We check the categories don't overlap, talk it through with you, and nothing appears on this page until both sides have agreed: a proposal is not an announcement, and we don't get to announce your involvement for you.

This goes to the same inbox as the site's feedback, and we answer wherever you tell us to. Your contact is used for that and nothing else — it is never shown on the site.
