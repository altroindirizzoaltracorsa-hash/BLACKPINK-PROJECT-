BLINKS UNITED · Team setup

Connect the Spotify scrobbler to your profile

A step-by-step guide to installing the SessionBox Multi-Account Scrobbler, linking it to your BLINKS UNITED account, and getting your plays to show on your profile and the leaderboard.

The whole thing takes about 5 minutes. You only do the setup once.

Before you start

  • A BLINKS UNITED account — the one you normally log in with (X, Google, Discord, or email).
  • The admin-panel key — ask whoever runs BLINKS UNITED for it. You need it once, to connect.
  • A Chromium browser: Brave, Chrome, or Edge.
  • SessionBox installed — only if you run more than one Spotify account at once.
  • The extension file (scrobbler-multi, version 1.4.0) — provided by your admin.

Setup

Install the extension

  1. Unzip the file. You'll get a folder called scrobbler-multi. Open it and confirm manifest.json sits directly inside (not in a nested folder).
  2. In your browser go to brave://extensions (or chrome://extensions / edge://extensions).
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top-right toggle).
  4. Click Load unpacked and select the scrobbler-multi folder.
  5. Check the card reads version 1.4.0.

Log in to BLINKS UNITED

Open blinksunited.com in the same browser and sign in with your usual account. Stay logged in — the next step needs your session.

Connect from the admin panel

  1. On the site, open the admin panel (the ⚙ button) and enter the admin key.
  2. Find the section 🎧 Connect Spotify extension.
  3. Click Connect my extension. A new tab opens and links your profile automatically — nothing to copy.
  4. Wait for ✅ Linked! Then close that tab.

Every Spotify tab in this browser now scrobbles to your profile — no matter how many accounts you run.

Stream on Spotify

Open your Spotify account(s) at open.spotify.com — use a separate SessionBox tab per account. Play the campaign tracks and let each one run.

  • A track counts once it has played ~60 seconds. Skips and ads don't count.
  • Keep the browser window visible (don't minimize it) — background tabs get paused by the browser.

Weak PC? Set Spotify's audio quality to Low (Settings → Audio quality). It has zero effect on whether a play counts, and lets you keep more tabs alive at once.

Check it's counting

Click the extension icon to open it. You'll see Scrobble activity ticking up, a chip per Spotify account, and — when you click an account — its per-song breakdown.

See it on your profile

Go to your BLINKS UNITED profile. Alongside your Last.fm / ListenBrainz rows you'll now see a Blinks United · Extension row with your total and a per-song breakdown:

Blinks United · Extension — last scrobble: 3m ago24 plays
↳ JUMP10
↳ Shut Down6
↳ GO5
↳ DDU-DU DDU-DU3

Your counts also fold into your leaderboard totals. The number settles on the next refresh (on submit, and hourly).

What counts

  • Campaign tracks only: JUMP · Shut Down · DDU-DU DDU-DU · GO · Less Than a Lover (Jennie).
  • Each play needs ~60 seconds of listening. Shorter plays and skips are ignored.
  • Ads don't count and never get sent.
  • Your plays land on your own profile — the extension row only ever shows your own data.

If something's off

The card doesn't say version 1.4.0
The browser loaded an old copy. Remove the extension, then Load unpacked the fresh scrobbler-multi folder again — and make sure manifest.json is directly inside the folder you pick (not one level up).
The "Connect my extension" tab doesn't say ✅ Linked!
Make sure the extension is installed and enabled in this browser, and that you're logged into BLINKS UNITED. Then reload the tab. If it still won't link, reopen the admin panel and click Connect my extension again.
Tracks play but the count doesn't move
A play only counts after ~60 seconds — quick skips won't register. Also keep the browser window visible; minimized windows pause the Spotify player. Open the extension to watch the activity update live.
The extension row isn't on my profile
Confirm you're logged in and viewing your own profile, then hit Refresh. The row only appears once you have at least one counted play. If it still doesn't show, tell your admin.

A note on fair play: driving plays across many accounts into one identity is against Spotify's and Last.fm's terms. These counts are self-reported to BLINKS UNITED's own leaderboard — they're a community tally, not an official streaming figure.
