The setup · 5 steps

From download to counting

1

Download it from the voting page

On blinksunited.com/voting (signed in, with a scrobbler linked) you'll see the VMA Vote Counter block. Tap Desktop for a computer, or Android for a phone. Then unzip the file you get.

2

Load it into your browser

Desktop — any Chromium browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi): open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode in the top-right, then click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder. Android — since Kiwi shut down, use a Chromium browser that still supports extensions: Microsoft Edge Canary (it inherited Kiwi's extension engine) or Quetta. Open its Extensions menu and load the .zip directly. Plain Chrome and Firefox for Android can't run it.

3

Link your Blinks United account

Click the extension's icon, then Link my Blinks United account. Sign in on the page that opens — the same account you use on the site (Google / X / Discord / magic link). The popup flips to ● Linked and shows your live count.

5

Watch your board climb

Back on /voting, your counted votes are already there — no manual entry. Keep the tab logic simple: stream + vote to rank on the leaderboard. During an MTV Power Hour or Double Day, the panel and popup glow gold to remind you votes count 2×.

Under the hood

What the extension does

🗳️
Auto-counts your votes

Reads each successful vote you cast on vote.mtv.com and logs the BLACKPINK/LISA ones to your board.

📊
Live on-page panel

A draggable, collapsible panel on the MTV site: today's total, a BLACKPINK / LISA split, and an activity log.

💗
“Blinks voting now”

A live pulse of how many blinks logged a vote in the last 90 seconds — our community, in real time.

⚡
Power-hour glow

Panel and popup light up gold during MTV Power Hours & Double Days, when votes count double.

⇄
Cross-device sync

Opt-in: merge your counts and the voting accounts you've used across every device you enable.

🛡️
Count-only, by design

It only observes votes you cast yourself — it never votes for you, so it stays within VMA rules.

🔒 Your privacy — does it take any private data? (tap to expand)

Sent to your board: your vote count + the BLACKPINK/LISA split, tied to your account by a random link token.

Never sent: your email, password, MTV login, or browsing data.

Kept on your device only: the accounts/emails you've voted with (for the 2/2 · 1/2 badges), your token, and the activity log.

Permissions — App: Internet only. Extension: storage + webRequest, restricted to vote.mtv.com and blinksunited.com (it can't see any other site).

It only reads the votes you cast — it never votes for you — and you sign in through your own browser, so the tool only ever receives a token, never your password.

It's open-source and installed outside the app stores, so it hasn't been through store review — the code is public to inspect, and you're trusting Blinks United.

🤝

Honour system, always

The counter reads only your own votes — it never casts one for you. Works on any desktop Chromium browser, or on Android through one that still supports extensions (Edge Canary / Quetta) — plain mobile Chrome and Firefox can't run it. If MTV changes its vote page for a future cycle, the extension may need a small update.
